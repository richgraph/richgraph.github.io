Richgraph Studio is the creator of the educational YouTube channels Richgraph (personal finance), Mindloop (psychology) and Factkeg (science facts).

Richgraph Studio Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's own original videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publish times.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's data.
