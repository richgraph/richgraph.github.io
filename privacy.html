Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Richgraph Studio Uploader ("the App") handles information. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the Richgraph, Mindloop and Factkeg YouTube channels.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

2. Information we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests permission to upload videos and manage video details (title, description, tags, thumbnails, captions, publish time) on the owner's own YouTube channels. The App does not read, collect or store any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

3. How information is used

Access is used only to upload and update the owner's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Storage

The Google authorization token and an upload log (video file name, video ID, title and date) are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on external servers by the App.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the local token file also removes all stored authorization.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions can be sent through the About page of youtube.com/@Richgraph.
